# Chat dashboard (lane 1 and lane 4)

Read before editing `src/shared/chat/dashboard/`, `src/views/sessions/chat-dashboard/`, `chat-view-pref.ts`, the Settings > Chat defaults dashboard rows, or `daemon/methods/quick_views.rs`. Design: `docs/chat-dashboard-design.md`.

- **Chat dashboard (lane 1 of docs/chat-dashboard-design.md).** Settings > Chat defaults > Chat view (`chatView` in settings.json's extra map, absent = Dashboard; the phone always draws Messages) picks what a chat window shows. The command palette's "Toggle Dashboard / Messages" row (shortcut id `toggle-chat-view`, no default keys, registered desktop-only in `sessions-dom-wiring.ts`) flips that same app-wide setting through `toggleChatView` in `chat-view-pref.ts`; it is not per chat (Joe, 2026-11-13). The model is `src/shared/chat/dashboard/dashboard-model.ts`: pure, it derives every zone from `ChatRenderer.messages` (send_message cards, question cards, `report_turn_status` rows, Joe's own messages) plus `ChatRenderer.dashPlans` (every `write_plan` of the chat, which draws no row; `remapIndices` keeps each plan's `at` and `lastAt` aligned). **To add a card, add a derive function and one entry to `ZONES` there (column, order, derive returning null when empty), then its renderer in `src/views/sessions/chat-dashboard/dashboard-zones.ts`'s `ZONE_VIEWS`, which is typed against `ZoneId` so a zone with no renderer fails typecheck.** Never touch the transcript renderer for a card. `chat-dashboard.ts` mounts beside `.session-messages` from `wireRenderer`, re-derives on `ChatRenderer.onFlushed`, redraws only zones whose HTML changed, and moves the chat's real status line (`.session-thinking`) into the Plan card's status slot while that card is drawn. The transcript keeps rendering underneath, hidden by `.session-pane[data-chat-view="dashboard"]`, so Messages view is byte-for-byte today's. View-harness `mountView` pins `chatView: "messages"` unless a spec passes `chatView: "dashboard"`. **The Answers card is one of two zones with their own DOM** (`chat-dashboard/answers-card.ts`): its zone renders a fixed `.ax-host` and the card draws inside it, so a re-derive never wipes an animation or a thread's message box. Its data is `src/shared/chat/dashboard/answers-model.ts` (only `answer`/`rating` messages count, plus a sub's finish card that carries a `question`: `spawn_chat` and `subchat send` take one, the daemon moves it from the sub's brief onto its next finish card (`finish::commit_finish_at`), and that card is the answer, so a subchats main chat never relays one; `send_message`'s answer-only `question` field is Claude's rewrite of Joe's question, falling back to his message). Read marks, thread links and + questions are daemon-side per chat (`ask/answer_state.rs`, `<data>/ask/<sid>.answers.json`, RPCs in `daemon/methods/answers.rs`, desktop-pipe only); a thread is an Ask thread, so follow-ups run the Ask sidecar with no spawn path of their own. History is the command palette with `remember: false`, `match` and `decorate` (`PaletteSources` in `palette-ui.ts`). **The Subchats card is the other one** (`chat-dashboard/subs-card.ts`, fixed `.sx-host`, LEFT column order 26 right under Waiting on you): a stuck sub (asking, paused, limited, stopped) is a tinted `.sx-item` row with one `subWhy` line (five rows, then "+N more"), a working sub an avatar in the `.sx-row` strip, and a click opens that sub's mini dashboard as a `SubPeek` (`sub-peek.ts`), a `position: fixed` card Joe drags by its top line; several stay open at once and each owns its composer (the chat's real `Composer`) and two tabs, Plan and Attachments. The Attachments tab is the dashboard's own four-tab card (Previews, Images, Files, Links) drawn by `attachTabs` in `dashboard-zones.ts` with `quiet` set (no heading, no explanatory text), fed by `shared/chat/dashboard/peek-attachments.ts`: previews filtered to that sub, images from `list_session_images` (picture reads wait for a drawn row), files and links scanned from the sub's last `HISTORY_MESSAGES` history events. The tab choice and the card's own tab survive the redraws. **A queued sub (held behind another, `progress.waiting_for`) has no process, so it is never a stuck row or a strip avatar** (Joe, 2026-11-18): `cardCounts` in `subs-model.ts` counts it apart as "N queued" (a chip beside the "N working · N asking · N stuck or waiting" words, hidden at 0), and clicking the chip opens `sub-queued-popup.ts` (`QueuedPopup`, the app's `openAnchoredPopover`, rows in start order from `queuedSubs`, "Starts after X" or "Starts after N chats" from `startsAfterText`, footnote `QUEUED_FOOTNOTE`). The popup patches in place on every refresh (a row that starts folds away at its `transitionend`, neighbours slide by FLIP, the popup never replays its enter, an empty list closes it); a row click is the avatar's `toggle` (it opens the peek and closes the popup, focus is not pulled back), a row right-click is the same chat menu, and the task line is one cached `get_sub_brief` per id. Space on the chip or on a popup row stops propagation because the composer's stray-input relay would otherwise type it into the composer (the same relay catches Space on any other focused button). The count and popup styles are `chat-dashboard/sub-queued-popup.css`. The peek shows the current plan step only (hover data), not the full step list, which would need a daemon read of the sub's transcript plan. its rules (who is listed, the one-hour fold of finished subs under "+N earlier" which is display only and never closes a sub) are the pure `shared/chat/dashboard/subs-model.ts`, its items come from the instance list through `chat-dashboard/subs-source.ts` (not from the transcript), and `syncInstances` pings `ChatDashboard.onSessionsChanged`. The avatar's right-click menu is `buildSubCardItems` (`chat-menu-items.ts`) drawn by `sub-card-menu.ts`. **Pin to sidemenu replaced Move to sidebar in the chat menu:** `Instance.pinned_to_sidebar` (registry snapshot, `#[serde(default)]`) is set by the `set_sub_pinned` RPC and only makes `sidebar-entries.ts` list the sub; the sub stays a sub (the daemon's `move_sub_to_sidebar` RPC is left but nothing in the UI calls it). A sub's images and brief come from `list_session_images` (history_scan's `facts_for`, ids read through `dashboard_gallery_image`) and `get_sub_brief` in `daemon/methods/sub_view.rs`. **Every model-and-effort mark is words:** `modelEffortTextHtml(model, effort)` writes them (`.session-model-text`); a new mark goes through that function.

- **Chat dashboard extras (lane 4).** The Attachments card (zone id `links`) and Changes are zones in the same `ZONES` table. Attachments is one card with four tabs, drawn in the order Previews, Images, Files and Links, the card opening on the first non-empty one at one fixed height on every tab, a file row showing the name only with the full path as its tooltip (`ATTACH_TABS`, `attachTabOf` in `dashboard-model.ts`; `deriveLinks` returns `{links, files, previews, images, tabs}`, and the Previews and Images tabs exist only while their section is on). There is no separate Preview zone. Links and files is derived on render (no Links store): `shared/chat/dashboard/links-model.ts` scans send_message text, details and card rows for URLs and file paths, plus `pr_preview` drafts and the Edit/Write tools' `file_path`; it draws with links, files, previews, images or once the chat has a plan, because it hosts the three quick-view buttons (Bigger plan, Decided, Project memory). Those call the desktop-pipe-only `chat_quick_view` RPC (`daemon/methods/quick_views.rs`, IPC wrapper `ipc/quick_views.rs`): `<cwd>/.claude/todos/PLAN.md` (else a plan `.md` the chat names, opened as a file ref), the main chat's subchats decisions log (`decisions::read_log`, a sub reads its parent's), and the Auto Memory folder (the session transcript's sibling `memory/`, else the cwd encoded as Claude Code names it). `chat-dashboard/quick-view.ts` is the read-only dialog, and `quick-views.ts` (`QuickViews`) holds the dashboard-side state of those buttons. The Previews tab (state and tiles in `preview-tiles.ts`, `PreviewTiles`; `derivePreviewWindow` still builds its state) reads `list_previews` scoped to the chat's family (`scopeRoot`), refreshed on the `preview` event, and lists only the previews whose `session_id` is this chat (a sub read in place: the sub) as rows, newest first, each with the page as a live scaled miniature; a row opens that preview in the Preview panel, capped at 8 with a "N more in the preview panel" row. The family's other previews sit behind an "N more by other chats" fold grouped by owner. The Images tab lists the Messages gallery's collection (`attachment-images.ts`, `AttachmentImages`: `collectChatImages` over the renderer's messages; the model carries only byte-free `DashImage` entries and the view points each `img[data-gimg]` at the bytes after the draw), newest first, capped at 12 with a "N more in the gallery" row; a row opens `openChatImageGallery` at that image, so the arrows from the gallery work. The card opens on Joe's last tab pick (`DashUi.linksTab`, in memory only) or else the first non-empty tab; empty tabs stay in the strip, muted (`.is-empty`), so it never reshuffles; the strip is `role=tablist` with roving tabindex and Arrow/Home/End keys (`tabKey`), the incoming list fades in once (`.is-in`, `playTabIn`), and Space on a card button is stopped before the composer's stray-input relay. A question's figures are the `visuals` of the own message sent just before it (`figuresBefore`). **Optional zones:** `SECTION_DEFAULTS` in `dashboard-model.ts` (links on, preview on, images on, changes off, plus drafts, picks and tests on; `links` hides the whole Attachments card, `preview` only its Previews tab, `images` only its Images tab) and Joe's overrides in settings.json's extra map under `dashboardSections` (`chat-view-pref.ts` `readSections`), switched in Settings > Chat defaults; a new optional card adds its id there and one row in `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS` in `chat-defaults.ts`. Extras CSS is `chat-dashboard/dashboard-extras.css`.

- **The pane's bottom edge** (Joe 2026-11-21). `.next-chip-slot` is `flex: none`: it scrolls sideways, which makes it a scroll container with an automatic min-height of 0, so a tall transcript or dashboard above it in `.session-pane`'s flex column used to shrink it until the chips were cut in half. `.cdash-body` has no bottom padding of its own (the attachments row's 104px in `composer.css` and the question card's inline `paddingBottom` still set one, and stack with the box); its `::after` is a 24px in-flow, sticky (`bottom: 0`) gradient to the pane colour, so a card that runs past the body's bottom edge (the sticky right column, a long left column) fades into the message box instead of a hard cut, and at the end of the scroll it sits past the last card over nothing. A gradient, not a `mask-image` on the body, because a mask would also fade the `position: fixed` drill popup. Proven by `e2e/view-harness/chat-bottom-edge.view.spec.ts` (chips whole at 1280, 900 and 390; the last pixel row at the edge is the pane colour on the void and void-light themes).

(1) The card's submit is ONE string, the note FIRST: `<auq-extra/>NOTE

(4) Liveness: a held prompt matched by what it asks goes to the NEWEST card asking it (an older twin was asked again and left behind); a card that grows old while the daemon is being read triggers another draw (`agedSince`); `clearUnreopenableCard` leaves a paired machine's chat alone. (5) `reconcile_pending_questions` (`daemon/state/pending_questions.rs`) also drops a restored card with definite proof it was settled while the daemon did not hear of it: a Skip mark in `companion.db` for that prompt id, or a user line in the chat's transcript whose `<auq-answer id="ID"/>` names it (`settled_card_ids`). A card with neither stays, and Joe's rule holds: a plain message beside a card never clears it, only an answer or a Skip. Not built, on purpose: a transcript-wide sweep of every chat at startup (opening a chat already runs the liveness read for its cards, and the daemon sweep covers the parked ones), and a registry of answers or skips that arrived before their card. "This chat is waiting on your answer" on the Plan card (`plan-card.ts`) is derived from `idleStatus`, which looks for an open question row, so it clears with the card.

## Chat heads (the chat list, pc-1769)

Read before editing `src/views/sessions/chat-heads*.ts`, `sidebar.ts`, `sidebar-ring.ts`, `src/shared/monogram.ts` `daemon/subchats/idle_freeze.rs` or `daemon/auto_hide.rs`. The left chat list (`.sessions-sidebar`, 260px) is gone. Chats are round **heads** stacked in the top-left corner of the chat view, under the app header. Joe's design is `docs/chat-heads-design.md`; this page is what a developer must know before editing the code. Todo: pc-1769.

### The DOM contract (about 160 view specs depend on it)

- The list is `<ul id="sessions-list" class="chat-heads-list">` inside `<nav id="chat-heads" class="chat-heads">`. It has **no** `sessions-list` class, so no old row CSS can match.
- A chat is `li.chat-head[data-session-id]`; a draft is `li[data-placeholder-id]`. Both carry `data-row-key`, `data-st`, `data-kbd-hint` (1-9, normal stack only), `data-head-kind`, `data-facts`, `aria-label`. The open chat has `li.active`.
- Control heads have a `data-row-key` of `__toggle`, `__back`, `__hidden`, `__new` and a `data-heads-*` attribute. The + head keeps the old FAB's id `sessionsFab`.
- `reconcileList` skips a row whose html did not change, so anything a runtime class needs (the keyboard highlight) is part of the rendered html, read from `headsUi`, never added to the DOM afterwards.

### Rules

- **Order**: age, newest at the far end, then Joe's manual order for what he placed. Nothing re-sorts on its own. The manual order is `chatHeadsOrder` in settings.json's extra map, written with `updateSettings` (`shared/settings-update`) on the desktop, so every window agrees. The phone is refused `save_settings`, so `storeOrder` (`chat-heads-drag.ts`) sends it the finished order through the `set_chat_heads_order` RPC (`daemon/methods/chat_heads_order.rs`, transport row `P`): one key, a list of at most 300 non-empty ids, validated and deduplicated; the daemon caches it and the app process persists it and emits `settings-changed` so the desktop windows redraw.
- **Open/closed** is `headsUi.open`, in memory per window, starting open. A phone tap on a head closes it; the toggle head reopens it. Android back from a chat with the stack closed opens the stack (`chat-heads-back` event, handled in `sessions-dom-wiring.ts`).
- **Marks**: ring = state (`sidebar-ring.ts`, now with `idle` and `remote` tones), bottom-right model and effort words (`modelEffortTextHtml`), bottom-left project badge (plain chats only), top-right red count. Everything else is in the hover card (`sidebar-hover-card.ts`, `factsFor`).
- **Hidden** chats leave the stack; the eye-slash head (with a count) shows them in place of the stack, the back head returns. The closed toggle's red number is the sum of the red numbers the heads draw (`HeadDraft.waiting` is taken from `headMark`), so a hidden chat (by hand, by the idle rule or put away) adds none: the collapsed button never shows a number the open stack and the hidden view cannot account for.
- **Ctrl+1..9** number `state.sortedSessionIds`, which `buildHeadEntries` sets to the normal stack's order. The palette (Ctrl+Shift+P) still finds every chat.
- **The open chat** is `li.active` and steps about 10px right with a white ring (`--head-ring-open`, a `--color-text` tint). The step, ring and shadow live on the `li` as `translate` and `box-shadow` transitions (200ms): the reconciler rewrites a head's insides on every update, so anything on the face could only appear, never animate. Hover nudges a head's FACE 4px right (not the `li`, or a pointer on its left edge would lose the hover and flicker), never scales it. The Ctrl highlight (`is-highlight`) steps the same way in `--color-primary`. Nothing reflows: only `translate`/`box-shadow` change, specs assert `offsetLeft/Top` are unchanged.
- **Ctrl numbers** are keycaps, the `::after` of every `li[data-kbd-hint]` (hidden at rest, so showing it is a transition and a reshuffle never replays it; `--i` from `buildHeadEntries` staggers 25ms per head on the way in). `::before` stays the drop-line. A Ctrl TAP (release before the 0.4s hold, no other key, click or blur) toggles `headsUi.open` (`chat-heads-keys.ts`); a hold keeps numbers, Up/Down and release-opens.
- **A project's main chat** is drawn as the project (`isProjectMainChat` in `chat-heads-model.ts`, shared by the head and the chat header): its head is the project face with no character, and `SessionHeader.bindSession` shows only that face and the project name (`.is-project-main`, the title node stays hidden for the title updates). Every other chat and every sub keeps the character header.
- No daemon connection and nothing to show: no heads at all (the pane shows the "Setting up" state).

### Freezing

Manual "Freeze chat" is gone (chat menu, palette, its coupling to Hidden). The sub "Pause" control stays, and so does the rate-limit auto-freeze.

The idle freeze (`idle_freeze.rs`) kills a chat's child and sets `frozen` + `auto_frozen`; `frozen_needs_continue` stays false, so waking never sends "continue". A chat qualifies only when `blocker()` finds nothing: interactive, not Jarvis or a worker, not a sub, not mirrored, not busy or compacting, `awaiting` none, `done` or `question` (a durable question card only; an open permission or secret card holds a live call and blocks, `holds_live_call`), no background task, no live sub, no held message, not closing, not under a live rate-limit window (a stamp whose reset time has passed no longer blocks: nothing clears the stamp on a timer). It must stay clear for 15 minutes (`IDLE_FREEZE_AFTER`, overridable with the `CC_IDLE_FREEZE_SECS` env var read once, on first use) by an in-memory first-seen clock and have an old `last_event_at`. A main chat past `IDLE_FLOOR_TOKENS` that idle compaction (`compaction::idle_compaction_pending`) has not yet run on is also blocked, since the compaction needs the live child at 30 quiet minutes; it is frozen 15 quiet minutes after the compaction.

`busy_watchdog.rs` is the 60 s tick; the sweep is called from `compaction::idle_sweep` so it adds no timer. The scheduler wakes an auto-frozen chat (`schedule_fire.rs` clears the pair before it fires). The front end wakes a chat when its head is opened (`selectSession` calls `unfreeze_session` silently) and on send (`sendBundle`).

**Which kind of freeze it is.** A usage-limit freeze and an idle freeze both read `frozen && auto_frozen`, and the limit stamp is wiped when any chat on the account completes a turn, so the front end cannot tell them apart from the flags. The daemon says so with `Instance.idle_frozen` (`Option<bool>`, `Some(true)` only while the sweep's freeze holds). `idle_freeze.rs` owns every write: `freeze` sets it with the stored mark (`idle-frozen<suffix>.json`), `wake` and `forget` clear both, `sync_markers` repairs drift on each sweep tick, and `persistence::populate_registry` restores it from the marks on boot. Every path that clears a freeze calls `forget` (`unfreeze_session`, `schedule_fire`), and the usage-limit freeze writer (`rate_limit.rs`) calls it too so an old mark cannot make a later limit freeze read as idle. `isIdleFrozen` and `needsFrozenLimitStrip` read the marker; an auto-freeze without it is a limit freeze (the strip shows, opening it shows the choice card). **A frozen chat that waits on a question card keeps its question state everywhere:** the head ring and count, and the dashboard's Plan card question lines all read `awaiting`/the transcript, never `frozen`. A new surface must not read `frozen` to mean "nothing to show". `e2e/view-harness/chat-heads-idle-frozen.view.spec.ts` pins the head and the question's card at desktop and phone width (its Waiting-card assertions predate the Plan card lines and still need updating), and that a card answered right after opening an idle-frozen chat is delivered once while the open's own un-awaited `unfreeze_session` is still pending (the answer does not depend on it).

**Not built (needs `daemon/lifecycle/spawn.rs`, the broker's area):** a spawn-without-message wake (opening an idle chat only clears the flags; the first send still pays the respawn) and a send landing in the up-to-5 s `kill_and_wait_for_teardown` window of the freeze (it writes to a child already marked `expected_exit`; the next send respawns it).

### Idle chats hide themselves

A chat that has done nothing for 10 minutes moves into the hidden window by itself, so the heads show only chats that are working or waiting on Joe. The rule lives in the daemon (`daemon/auto_hide.rs`), not the front end, because the hidden list is the daemon's (`sessions/hidden_chats.rs`, shared by the desktop and the phone) and the checks need the registry, the viewed marks, the card list and the drafts store. The front end's only change is the Settings switch. A hide arrives as the existing `hidden_chats_changed` push, `hidden-sessions-sync.ts` adopts it and `renderSidebar` draws the head leaving with the same exit motion as a hand-hide (`row-exiting`); a chat coming back enters with `row-entering`. A hidden chat draws no number (`headMark`), the eye head's count grows, and the toggle's red count still includes a hidden chat's card.

**Idle** is `auto_hide::blocker()` finding nothing: interactive, not Jarvis or a worker, not a sub, not mirrored, not `pinned_to_sidebar`, not ended or closing; not `busy` or compacting; `awaiting` is none or `done` (a question, permission or waiting state is "not idle", unlike the idle freeze, which lets a durable question through); no background task, no held message, no open card (`lifecycle::has_open_prompt`, any card); no sub that has not finished, and none that finished after Joe last viewed the main chat or the sub; no open draft for the chat (or for its subs, as the heads' pencil counts them); and **Joe has viewed its latest result**: `last_event_at` is not after the chat's `viewed_at_ms` (`sessions/chat_config.rs`). A chat Joe never opens after its result therefore never hides, on purpose. `last_event_at` is not stored, so a restart (an app update) restores every row without it: a row with no stamp counts as unviewed until Joe has viewed the chat since the daemon started (`auto_hide::unviewed`, `Facts.boot_ms`; his open window renews the mark every minute, so a chat he is reading clears at once, and a chat he does not open after an update does not hide until he does), and an empty stamp is no change for a chat the rule hid or Joe showed by hand, so a restart neither un-hides nor re-hides anything. The clock is the in-memory first-seen clock (as the freeze) plus the wall clock from `max(last_event_at, viewed_at_ms)`, so a chat Joe has open never hides: his window renews the open chat's mark every minute while it shows (`chat-seen.ts` `startViewedHeartbeat`, started by `sidebar.ts`), because reading sends no other event. The heartbeat stops when Joe leaves the Sessions view (`teardownState`); a window left showing a chat while Joe is away therefore keeps that chat awake. The sweep rides `busy_watchdog`'s 60 s tick, after `compaction::idle_sweep`; the switch is Settings, Chat defaults, "Hide idle chats" (`autoHideIdleChats`, on when absent, read by `Settings::auto_hide_idle_chats`). Off, the next `reconcile` brings back every chat the rule hid.

**It never overrules Joe, and it records what it did.** `sessions/auto_hidden.rs` (`auto-hidden-chats.json`) holds `hidden` (chats the rule hid) and `shown` (chats Joe showed by hand), each with the chat's `last_event_at` at the time (`hidden` is never capped, `shown` keeps the newest 500 ids; `last_event_at` moves when the busy state flips and on any line the chat's child prints). The id also goes in `hidden-chats.json`, so every surface that reads the hidden list (sidebar section, palette, phone) treats it as hidden with no change. The one hook for Joe's hand is `auto_hide::note_hand_change`, called by `update_hidden_chats` for every delta (desktop, phone and peer alike) after the peer calls succeed and before the local list changes: a hide by hand drops the chat from the rule's `hidden` (it is Joe's from then on, and the rule never brings it back); a show by hand writes `shown`, and the rule skips that chat until its `last_event_at` moves (a new turn), then forgets the choice. **The flag is `auto_hidden`, deliberately not `hidden`:** the durable `hidden` flag of the put-away chain (pc-1806) is a different thing, and neither may read the other's store.

**Coming back.** `auto_hide::reconcile` runs from `publish_instances_changed` (every registry change), so a card, a new turn, a queued message or a sub starting brings the chat back at once, not at the next tick: `busy` (unless a compaction just ended, see below), `awaiting` not none/done, a background task, `held_count > 0`, a sub that has not finished, or a `last_event_at` different from the one stored when it was hidden. The daemon's own idle compaction is housekeeping: while `compaction::is_compacting` nothing comes back, and within a minute of `compaction::ended_within` a busy flag counts for nothing (so a real turn Joe starts in that minute brings the chat back at the next 60 s sweep, not at once) and a stamp that moved only refreshes the stored one (`Verdict::Refresh`), since the compaction's end stamps `last_event_at` after its flight is cleared. A finished sub on a paired machine carries no finish time here, so it never counts as unviewed. The mirrored chats of a paired machine are that machine's to hide.
